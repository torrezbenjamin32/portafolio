Chassis Project

Desafío Eco 2026 · Instituto Madero

1. Design & Cutting

Steel tubes Measurement Regulations

We designed the chassis and cut the structural tubes following safety regulations. We used a level, square, and tape measure to ensure perfect symmetry from the start.

2. Welding & Assembly

MIG welding Roll cage Seat

We welded the main structure and the safety roll bars. We installed the bucket seat and mounted the 4 wheels to verify the stability and overall geometry of the vehicle.

3. Current Status

Electric motor Batteries Traction

The chassis is welded and standing on its own wheels. The next step is installing the electrical system, motor, and batteries, followed by the bodywork and final testing.
